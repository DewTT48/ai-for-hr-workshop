What format should the answer take?

6
Guardrail

What must AI not do?

Why criteria matters most

Without criteria

AI often gives generic recommendations that sound good but do not match the HR problem.

With criteria

AI can compare, prioritize, and explain why an HR action or risk area matters.

Examples of HR criteria: performance evidence, competency framework, role requirement, business impact, urgency, fairness, risk level, data quality.

RESPONSIBLE AI FOR HR

AI can prepare. HR decides.

Good use
  • Organize evidence
  • Compare against criteria
  • Suggest questions
  • Highlight missing data
  • Prepare discussion options
Risky use
  • Decide promotion
  • Assign rating
  • Select final candidate
  • Invent missing facts
  • Use identifiable personal data carelessly

DASHBOARD THINKING

A dashboard starts with a decision, not a chart

Ask before creating indicators
  1. What decision needs support?
  2. Who will use the dashboard?
  3. What risk or opportunity are we monitoring?
  4. What data source is available?
  5. What HR action should follow?
Examples
  • Low engagement → Engagement Index → investigate root causes
  • Low management trust → Management Trust Score → manager enablement
  • Low training relevance → Training Relevance Score → redesign TNA
  • Low IDP progress → IDP Milestone Completion → manager follow-up

OUTPUT REVIEW

Before using AI output, check 5 things

Quality checks
  1. Is the output based on the data provided?
  2. Did AI invent or overgeneralize?
  3. Is there evidence for each recommendation?
  4. Is the output usable for the HR audience?
  5. Is human review clearly required before action?
Mission-specific checks
  • Communication: Is the CTA clear?
  • Survey / Dashboard: Are indicators tied to data sources and actions?
  • IDP: Does the plan include exposure and practice, not just training?
The quality of HR AI work depends not only on the prompt, but also on how HR reviews the output.
